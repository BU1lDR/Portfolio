Security Scanner

              
A CLI — secscan — that points three kinds of
                check at one target and reconciles the results: dependency CVEs
                from OSV.dev, source patterns like eval and hardcoded keys, and
                live-site TLS, headers, cookies and exposed files. Passive by
                default; the intrusive probes stay off until you both ask for them
                and confirm you’re authorized to test the host.

              
  • Python
  • asyncio
  • httpx
  • cryptography
  • pytest

Retail Customer Segmentation

              
The IBM SkillsBuild × BharatCares capstone — an
                end-to-end analysis of 1,067,371 UCI Online Retail II transaction
                lines in a single notebook: a cleaning audit that accounts for every
                row it drops, RFM segmentation cross-checked against K-Means, CLV
                estimation and cohort retention. It identifies 683 high-value
                accounts, £1.69M of revenue, that had stopped ordering.

              
  • Python
  • pandas
  • NumPy
  • scikit-learn
  • Matplotlib
  • Jupyter
